About

Be who you are, out loud.

Umbrella4U is a youth-led movement for self-expression, a place to break the social norm, drop the mask, and be accepted for exactly who you are. It starts with Haven, an anonymous space where your voice is heard.

Why we exist

Fitting in shouldn't cost you yourself.

Too many young people shrink themselves to match what's "normal", quieter, cooler, smaller, just to avoid being judged.

A lot of us grew up getting labeled for being different, and learning that the safest move was to hide who we really are. But hiding is exhausting, and it's lonely. The world doesn't need you to fit a mold. It needs you, fully yourself.

So we're building a place with no mask required and no "normal" to perform, just room to express who you actually are and be met with acceptance instead of judgment, by people who get it.

Our story

It started with one stubborn belief.

That you should never have to be the "typical" anyone to be accepted. That belief became Haven, an anonymous space where young people say what's really on their mind, in their own voice, and get met with acceptance instead of judgment. People used it. It helped. And it made one thing clear: the need is far bigger than one app.

So we built the organization around it. Umbrella4U is the home for Haven and the products that follow it, each designed with young people, held to a real standard of safety, and free where it matters most.

An umbrella shelters one person. We're trying to hold one over a whole generation, so no one has to hide under it.

What Haven is

A safe place to be heard when it's heavy.

Haven is our flagship product: anonymous peer support for young people. When you're carrying something, you set it down here and get met with warmth, not judgment, from people who understand because they've been there too. Here's how it works:

☂ UmbrellasWhen a story moves you, you open an umbrella on it, a quiet "I'm with you." No performance, no comment threads to manage. The umbrella is the message.
🌦️ Your weatherWhen you post, you choose your weather, from a clear sky to a heavy storm. As umbrellas come in, your weather clears toward sunny. Support you can actually watch arrive.
Four ways to be metPost as Listen, Advice, Similar, or Resources, so you get exactly the kind of response you need, and nothing you don't.
Filters that protect youSafety and privacy filters keep it anonymous and kind, no names, no contact info, no cruelty.

The bigger picture

One umbrella, many tools.

Haven is the first product, not the last. Umbrella4U is building a family of tools that each solve a different piece of the same problem, getting a young person from "I can't say this out loud" to "someone's got me."

In progress: Lighthouse (the fastest calm path to real crisis help), Almanac (a private journal that turns hard days into patterns you can see), and Greenhouse (short, guided skills for hard moments). More are on the way, peer groups, tools for schools and families, and in-person community.

What we believe

The things we won't compromise on.

Youth-first, always

Co-designed with the people who use it. If it doesn't feel safe to a 15-year-old, it doesn't ship.

Free where it counts

Any young person gets full access for free, forever. Schools and systems fund the rest.

Safety is the product

Human moderation, crisis pathways, and privacy by default, not an afterthought bolted on later.

Come as you are

No mask, no "normal" to perform. However you show up, you belong under the umbrella.

Safety & privacy

Built so you can open up without opening yourself up.

  • Anonymous by default, no real names, and posts you make anonymously aren't tied to your public identity.
  • Your data is yours, protected per-user, encrypted in transit, never sold, no ads.
  • Human moderation, real people keep the space kind, backed by content and privacy filters.
  • Crisis pathways, if a post sounds like an emergency, Haven points to trained help right away (988, Kids Help Phone) instead of pretending peer support is enough.
