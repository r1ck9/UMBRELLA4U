What is Umbrella4U?

A youth-led movement for self-expression, helping young people be who they really are and break the social norms that say they can't.

We're a non-profit, started by students and run by a volunteer team with adult guidance. We build free, judgment-free spaces where young people can drop the mask and be accepted for exactly who they are, then spread them city by city through the young people who lead our Chapters.

  • Non-profit · free where it counts, forever
  • Youth-led · built with the people who use it
  • Judgment-free · come as you are
  • A shelter of tools · Haven today, more on the way
  • Chapter-powered · student & community led
  • Across North America · and growing

Who we are

We started with one stubborn belief: no young person should have to hide who they are, or shrink to fit someone else's idea of "normal," just to be accepted.

That belief became Haven, an anonymous space where teens say what's really on their mind and get met with warmth instead of judgment, a place to feel less alone. It works. Now we're building the rest of the shelter around it.

Umbrella4U is the movement behind Haven and the products that follow it. Different tools, one umbrella: each designed with young people, held to a real standard of safety, and free at the point where it matters most, for the person who needs it.

Meet Haven ☂

Haven is our anonymous peer-support app, a safe place to be heard when you're carrying something heavy. Share what's weighing on you and get met with warmth, not judgment, from people who understand because they've been there too. When a story moves someone, they open one umbrella for you, a quiet "I'm standing with you." No pile-ons, no arguing, no names, just support, so no one has to face it alone.

  • No name required
  • One umbrella each
  • Human moderation
  • Crisis pathways

Lead a Chapter

A Chapter is a small team that brings Umbrella4U to life on the ground, running peer support, events, and outreach where they live. Roles per Chapter:

  • Chapter Lead, runs the Chapter and is its point person.
  • Co-Lead / VP, shares the load and steps in when needed.
  • Peer Support Lead, trains and coordinates the listeners.
  • Wellbeing & Safety Officer, owns escalation; the calm head in a hard moment.
  • Outreach Lead, brings new members and volunteers in.
  • Events Lead, runs meetups and awareness weeks.
  • Partnerships Liaison, the bridge to school admin and counselors.
  • Comms Lead, social media and telling the Chapter's story.
  • Treasurer, local fundraising and a small budget.

Regional & national · build the org

Join the core team

Beyond individual Chapters, a regional and national team makes the whole network work, and keeps it safe. Leadership roles:

  • Regional Director, grows and supports Chapters across a region.
  • Chapter Success Manager, onboards new Chapters and keeps them healthy.
  • Head of Safety & Clinical, protocols, crisis standards, and duty of care.
  • Head of Training, the curriculum every leader completes.
  • Head of Partnerships, schools, districts, and health systems.
  • Head of Fundraising, grants, donors, and sponsored seats.
  • Head of Comms & Marketing, the story, the brand, the reach.
  • Head of People & Volunteers, recruiting, onboarding, recognition.
  • Equity & Belonging Lead, making sure Umbrella4U is for everyone.
