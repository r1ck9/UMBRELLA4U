What is Umbrella4U?

A non-profit run by students, building free places where young people don't have to perform.

We started as a student project and we're still run by volunteers, with adults advising rather than deciding what happens. Everything we build stays free for the people who use it, and it spreads through Chapters, which are small teams running things locally in a school or a city.

  • Non-profit · free where it counts, forever
  • Youth-led · built with the people who use it
  • Judgment-free · come as you are
  • One app · Haven, free and anonymous
  • Chapter-powered · student & community led
  • Across North America · and growing

Who we are

The thinking behind it isn't complicated: nobody should have to edit themselves down to a smaller, safer version just to be accepted by the people around them.

So we built Haven, an anonymous app where teenagers post what's actually on their mind and get met with something other than judgment. It's the part of all this that exists today, and it's where most of our attention goes.

Umbrella4U is the organisation that grew up around Haven and the tools we want to build after it. All of it gets designed with the people who'll actually use it, and it stays free for them, because the money that keeps it running should come from schools and funders rather than from teenagers.

1

Youth-first

Co-designed with the people who use it. If a 15-year-old doesn't feel safe using it, we don't ship it.

2

Free for young people

Any young person gets the whole thing at no cost, with nothing that expires or asks for a card. Schools and funders cover what it costs to run.

3

Moderation and crisis paths

There are human moderators, a route out to real help, and privacy switched on by default. We built all of that in at the start, because it's very hard to bolt on once a community already exists.

4

Meant to spread

One organisation and several tools, run locally by Chapters instead of out of a single office somewhere.

What we've built · live today

Meet Haven ☂

Haven is our anonymous peer-support app. You post whatever is weighing on you, and it gets read by people who have been somewhere similar. When a story lands with someone, they open one umbrella for you, which is a quiet way of saying they're standing with you. There's no comment box anywhere in it, so there's nothing waiting underneath a post for you to dread.

  • No name required
  • One umbrella each
  • Human moderation

Lead a Chapter

A Chapter runs peer support, events and outreach in its own school or city. Most fill some version of the roles below, and smaller ones double up until there are enough people to go round.

  • Chapter Lead · runs the Chapter and is its point person.
  • Co-Lead / VP · shares the load and steps in when needed.
  • Peer Support Lead · trains and coordinates the listeners.
  • Wellbeing & Safety Officer · owns escalation; the calm head in a hard moment.
  • Outreach Lead · brings new members and volunteers in.
  • Events Lead · runs meetups and awareness weeks.
  • Partnerships Liaison · the bridge to school admin and counselors.
  • Comms Lead · social media and telling the Chapter's story.
  • Treasurer · local fundraising and a small budget.

Regional & national · build the org

Join the core team

These roles sit above Chapter level, and between them they keep the wider network running and safe.

  • Regional Director · grows and supports Chapters across a region.
  • Chapter Success Manager · onboards new Chapters and keeps them healthy.
  • Head of Safety & Clinical · protocols, crisis standards, and duty of care.
  • Head of Training · the curriculum every leader completes.
  • Head of Partnerships · schools, districts, and health systems.
  • Head of Fundraising · grants, donors, and sponsored seats.
  • Head of Comms & Marketing · the story, the brand, the reach.
  • Head of People & Volunteers · recruiting, onboarding, recognition.
  • Equity & Belonging Lead · making sure Umbrella4U is for everyone.
