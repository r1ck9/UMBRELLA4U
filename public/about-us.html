Who we are

The people behind Umbrella4U.

A small, youth-led team of students, builders, and advisors, all working so no young person has to face the hard stuff alone.

Ricky Chen
Founder · CEO · CTO

I'm Ricky, a 15-year-old student entering Grade 10 at St. George's School in Vancouver and the founder of Umbrella4U. I'm passionate about challenging the stereotypes and social norms that stop young people from expressing who they truly are. Growing up, I often got labeled as "feminine," "gay," "zesty", "weird", etc. I know what it feels like to be judged for simply being yourself. Those experiences inspired me to start Umbrella4U. My hope is simple: to help build a world where no one feels alone or pressured to hide who they are. You may know me as a volleyball player, a swimmer, an amateur pianist, an anime enthusiast, or even a mediocre cosplayer. But this time, I want to be someone that can change the world for the better. Once and for All.
