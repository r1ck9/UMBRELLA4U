Who we are

The people behind Umbrella4U.

A small, youth-led team of students, builders, and advisors, all working so no young person has to face the hard stuff alone.

Ricky Chen
Founder · CEO · CTO

I'm Ricky, a 15-year-old student entering Grade 10 at St. George's School in Vancouver and the founder of Umbrella4U. I'm passionate about challenging the stereotypes and social norms that stop young people from expressing who they truly are. Growing up, I never quite fit the social norm, I wasn't the "typical" boy people expected me to be, and I got judged for it, labeled "feminine," "gay," "zesty," "weird," and more. I know what it feels like to be looked at differently for simply being yourself. Those experiences inspired me to start Umbrella4U. My hope is simple: to help build a world where no one feels alone or pressured to hide who they are. You may know me as a volleyball player, a swimmer, an amateur pianist, an anime enthusiast, or even a mediocre cosplayer. But this time, I want to be someone that can change the world for the better, and make acceptance the norm.

Kaylee
Art Director · Washington Chapter President

Hi, I'm Kaylee! I'm 15, and I go to Mercer Island High School in Washington. I love all forms of art, volleyball, music, my friends, and learning new things. I lead the Umbrella4U Chapter in Washington, and I hope that this organization reaches the people who need it. I'm not comfortable being vulnerable because it was always easier figuring things out myself. I didn't have the resources to help me develop good coping mechanisms, but a few simple words from others helped me break them. Because of this, my goal is to spread that same support to as many people as I can.
